Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Liftum (“the app”) is a workout tracking app made by independent developer Oytun Emre Özmel (“we”). This policy explains what personal data the app processes, why and how. The data controller is Oytun Emre Özmel.

In short

  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising
  • Your data is stored in the EU (Germany)
  • You can delete your account and all your data in the app

1. Information we collect

You need an account to use Liftum. With your account we store:

  • Account: your first and last name, email address and password. Your password is stored hashed; we can’t see it.
  • Profile: the age, gender, height, weight, goal and activity level you enter during setup.
  • Workout data: your programs, workout days and exercises, logged sets (weight, reps, duration, effort), workout sessions, day notes, exercise goals and exercises you add yourself.
  • Body measurements (optional): weight, body fat and waist, chest, arm, hip and thigh circumference.
  • Settings: units (kg/lb, cm/in), theme, rest times and notification preferences.

Height and weight in your profile and your body measurements may count as health-related data. We use them only to show you your progress, your records and the calculations in the app. Entering body measurements is optional.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your language and theme preference, a copy of your settings and your sign-in session are stored on your device. Rest timer notifications are scheduled on your device, and the live timer on the Lock Screen (Live Activity) also runs on your device. No extra data is sent to us for these.

3. How we use information

We use your information only to run the app: to create and protect your account, save your workouts and measurements, show your progress and records, send account emails such as password resets, and answer your support requests.

We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising or profiling. The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools.

4. Program sharing

When you share a program with a share code, anyone who enters the code sees the program’s contents and your first and last name, and can add a copy of the program to their own account. Your workout logs and measurements are not shared. Only you can start a share.

5. Service providers

We use the following service providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf and to provide the service.

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.): database and account infrastructure; also sends account emails. Your data is stored on Supabase servers in Frankfurt, Germany. Privacy policy
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.): delivers app updates to your device. Technical information such as your device’s IP address and app version is processed for this. Privacy policy
  • Apple: app distribution and subscription payments. Your payment details never reach us; we can only see the status of your subscription (whether it is active). Privacy policy
  • Google (Gmail): when you email us, the conversation goes through Gmail. Privacy policy

6. International transfers

Your data is stored on servers in Germany (EU). If you live outside the EU, for example in Turkey, this means your data is transferred abroad. Expo, Apple and Google are US-based companies; some technical data may be processed in the United States when these services are used.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is open. You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your account and all of your workout, measurement, note and settings data are permanently deleted. Deleted data also leaves our infrastructure provider’s technical backups once the backup cycle completes.

Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store subscription. You can cancel it on your iPhone under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

8. Security

All connections between the app and the server are encrypted (HTTPS). In the database, each user can access only their own data. No system is completely secure; if a data breach occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority within the legal time limits.

9. Children

Liftum is not designed for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don’t knowingly collect personal data from them. If we learn that such an account has been created, we will delete the account and its data.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, or Article 11 of Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, KVKK), you have the right to:

  1. afind out whether your personal data is processed, and request information about it;
  2. blearn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used for that purpose;
  3. cknow the third parties, in your country or abroad, to whom your data is transferred;
  4. dask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected;
  5. eask for your data to be deleted or destroyed;
  6. fask for any correction or deletion to be passed on to those third parties;
  7. gobject to a decision against you that is based solely on automated processing;
  8. hclaim compensation if you suffer damage because your data was processed unlawfully;
  9. ilodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Board).

You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account. For requests made by email, we ask that they come from the email address registered to your account; if you write from another address, we will send a confirmation to your registered address. We won’t ask for copies of ID documents.

How to make a request: email oytunemre.apps@gmail.com. We will reply free of charge within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, if a new service provider is added), we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
