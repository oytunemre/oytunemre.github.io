Getting started

  1. 1Create an account and enter your height, weight and goal during setup.
  2. 2In the Program tab, create a program and add exercises to its days.
  3. 3Open a day and tap Start workout. When you finish a set, enter the weight and reps and save it; the rest timer starts on its own.
  4. 4Follow your progress and records in Profile, and your body measurements in the Measure tab.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your account’s email address. A reset link is sent to that address.

Can I use pounds (lb) instead of kilograms?

Yes. Under Profile → Settings → Units you can choose units for weight (kg/lb), length (cm/in) and distance (km/mi). Your past logs don’t change; only how they are shown.

I don’t get the rest notification.

Check that Rest notification is on under Profile → Settings, and that notifications are allowed for Liftum in your iPhone’s Settings → Liftum → Notifications. A Focus mode (such as Do Not Disturb) can silence notifications.

The timer doesn’t show on my Lock Screen.

Check that Live timer on lock screen is on under Profile → Settings, and that Live Activities are allowed in your iPhone’s Settings → Liftum. The timer appears once you start a workout.

Will I lose my data if I change phones?

No. Your programs, logs and measurements are stored in your account. Just sign in on the new phone with the same email and password.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Liftum and cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, tap Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your account and all of your workout, measurement, note and settings data are permanently deleted; this can’t be undone. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
