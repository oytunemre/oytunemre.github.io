Liftum

Workout tracker

Build your training program, log every set, and see your records and progress. Quick logging at the gym, a clear picture of your progress afterwards.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. App language: Turkish (English coming soon).

Overview Privacy Terms Support

Features

  • Your own program

    Build your program; log the weight, reps and rest for every set.

  • Supersets and drop sets

    Group exercises into supersets, tri-sets or giant sets; log drop sets.

  • Progress and records

    Personal records, estimated one-rep max, progress charts and deload suggestions.

  • Timer on your Lock Screen

    Your rest timer shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

  • Body measurements

    Log your weight, body fat and circumferences, and compare them over time.

  • No ads, no tracking

    Your data is stored in the EU, and you can delete your account at any time.
