Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Apex Echo (“the app”) is a game made by Oytun Emre Özmel, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: we don’t collect personal data.

In short

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • We don’t sell or share personal data
  • Your progress stays on your device

1. Information stored on your device

Your best score, best combo, coins, unlocked characters and maps, and settings (sound and vibration) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also reset your best score from the app’s settings.

2. Online co-op (optional)

When you create or join a room, or use Quick Match, the app connects to our game server so that players can race in real time. During a match the app sends:

  • a random player ID that is created each time the app starts (it is not linked to you, your device or any account);
  • your selected character;
  • your in-game position, height and score;
  • the room code.

This information is shared live only with the other players in the same match. It is held in the server’s memory only while the match is running and is then discarded. It is not saved to a database or log, and it is not used for any other purpose. Other players see you only as “PLAYER 2”, “PLAYER 3” and so on. There are no names, profiles or chat.

3. Service provider

Our game server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which operates data centers around the world. As with any internet connection, Cloudflare processes your device’s IP address to deliver the connection. We do not receive or store it. You can read Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

4. Children

Apex Echo is suitable for all ages. It has no chat, no user profiles and no way to share personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, or Article 11 of Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, KVKK), you have the right to:

  1. afind out whether your personal data is processed, and request information about it;
  2. blearn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used for that purpose;
  3. cknow the third parties, in your country or abroad, to whom your data is transferred;
  4. dask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected;
  5. eask for your data to be deleted or destroyed;
  6. fask for any correction or deletion to be passed on to those third parties;
  7. gobject to a decision against you that is based solely on automated processing;
  8. hclaim compensation if you suffer damage because your data was processed unlawfully;
  9. ilodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Board).

Because we don’t store personal data about you, there is usually nothing to access, correct or delete. Game data on your device can be removed by deleting the app.

How to make a request: email oytunemre.apps@gmail.com. We will reply free of charge within 30 days.

6. International use

The app can be played worldwide. Co-op traffic passes through Cloudflare’s global network and may be handled in data centers outside your country, only for the duration of the match.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, if we add ads or in-app purchases in a future version), we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
