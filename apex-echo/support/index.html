How to play

Use the left and right buttons to move and JUMP to leap from platform to platform. Hold JUMP to keep jumping automatically, and tap it again to stop. Skip several floors in one jump to build a combo. Climb as high as you can before you fall off the screen.

Co-op (2–5 players)

  • Play with friends: one player creates a room and shares the 6-character room code. The others join by entering the code. The host taps START once 2 or more players are in. A full room of 5 starts automatically.
  • Quick Match: matches you with other players from around the world.
  • Winning: the last player standing wins. If everyone falls, the highest climber wins. Only the winner earns coins.

FAQ

Do I need an internet connection?
Only for co-op. Solo play works offline.

My progress disappeared.
Progress (best score, coins, unlocked items) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes it.

“Room not found”.
Check the code. A room closes when everyone has left it.

I can’t connect to co-op.
Check your internet connection. Some school or office Wi-Fi networks block real-time game connections. Try mobile data.

I left the app during a match and lost.
To keep matches fair, a player who leaves the app or loses connection for a few seconds is knocked out.
